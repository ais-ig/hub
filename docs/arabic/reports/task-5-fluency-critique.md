# Task 5 · Fluency critique of the Arabic

Read on 30 September 2026 as a Saudi parent would: the Arabic alone first, in page order (337 dictionary strings plus the ten update notices), then against the English only to keep each proposed rewording faithful. Accuracy against the English was not checked here.

## 1. Overall impression

This reads, for the most part, as Arabic written by a school and not as a translation. The voice is steady from top to bottom: the respectful plural is held everywhere, imperatives are consistently in the second person plural, tanween sits on the alif throughout, Arabic commas and question marks are used, and I could not tell where one translator stopped and the next began. The school vocabulary is largely the vocabulary a Saudi parent already knows (رائد الفصل، حصة الريادة، الفسحة، المرشد الطلابي، مشرف الدور، منسوبي القسم، تحويلة، نموذج التعهد، عقد سلوكي، يُعمل بها اعتباراً من). The best sections are the hero and "Start here", the two-year pathway (s2), the options form instructions, and the mentor programme. The weakest are the behaviour block (s7), where the English noun fragments were carried over as fragments, the "Beyond the books" lead, and the support section wherever "intervention" appears as التدخل. The update notices are clear, but the most important one has a sentence a parent will read two ways. I would be comfortable for the school to publish this once findings 1 to 12 are applied; the rest are polish.

Two things are outside my brief but I tripped on them as a reader, so they go to the accuracy reviewer: (a) `s3.short` says "تختارون بين اللغة العربية والمحاسبة" while the table directly below lists اللغة العربية as compulsory, which a parent will read as a contradiction unless the optional one is named as IGCSE Arabic; (b) `s6.allyear` "مسابقة ملتقى الصلاة" is not a phrase I recognise, and the school surely has its own Arabic name for that competition.

## 4. Rules and glossary terms I would challenge

These are not applied in the findings above, because the rules forbid it.

1. **سياسة منع الهواتف المحمولة / الهاتف المحمول.** No Saudi parent says الهاتف المحمول; they say الجوال, and so do Saudi schools in their own circulars (منع الجوال، حجز الجوال). "الهاتف" also collides inside one sentence in `s7.attendance.li2`, where "عبر الهاتف" means a phone call and two lines later "الهواتف" means the device. I would change the glossary to سياسة منع الجوال and use الجوال / الجوالات in `s7.phones.title`, `s7.phones.li1`, `s7.phones.li2`, `s7.phones.th.held`, `doc.phonepolicy.t`, `doc.commitment.t`, `doc.commitment.sb`, keeping "عبر الهاتف" in `s7.attendance.li2` for the call. Check first what the Arabic side of the school's own policy PDF says. The choice of "حجز" for confiscation, by contrast, is right and should stay: it says temporary, where مصادرة says permanent.
2. **مقر البنين.** Understood, but not what families say; Saudi schools write قسم البنين or مبنى البنين. Since قسم is taken by القسم البريطاني, مبنى البنين would be my choice. This is the school's name for its own site, so the school's signage and letterhead should decide.
3. **تحديد كمقروء.** "تحديد" is "select". The phones in parents' hands say تمييز كمقروء (iOS, WhatsApp). I would use تمييز كمقروء and تمييز الكل كمقروء in `strip.mark` and `bell.markAll`.
4. **"Every number appears with the same digits" applied to spelled-out percentages.** Because the English spells some percentages and not others, the Arabic says "ستين في المئة" in `s4.progress.li3` and "60%" in `s5.early.p` for the same threshold, and "خمسين في المئة" and "أربعون في المئة" sit a screen away from "100%". A parent scanning for the pass line finds digits faster. I would write 60%, 50%, 40% in `s4.lead`, `s4.progress.li3`, `s3.equiv.note`, or fix the English so both agree.
5. **Grade numbers.** The glossary gives الصف العاشر for one grade and digits for a range. The Student Council notice writes "ممثل الصف 10" and "من الصف 12" for single grades. It is readable, and spelling them out beside "الصفوف 9 و11 و12" would look worse, so I would add "digits when grades are listed together in one notice" to the rule instead of changing the text.
6. **المرشد الطلابي.** Correct for parents. The Ministry now uses الموجّه الطلابي officially; if the school's own Arabic documents have moved to it, follow them.
7. **الربع.** Saudi government schools say الفترة; international schools do say الربع and the reports carry it, so keep it. Noted only so nobody "corrects" it later.
8. **Academic year written three ways** (`2026/27`, `2026-2027`, `2026/2027`). This mirrors the English, but in Arabic the hyphenated form is also the one most likely to reorder. One form, `2026/27`, everywhere would be cleaner.
9. **Countdown units** (`hero.cd.days` يوم, ساعة, دقيقة, ثانية). Singular under any number is not grammatical agreement (3 يوم), but it is the convention on every Arabic countdown and changing by number would need code. Leave it.

## 5. What reads well

- رائد الفصل and حصة الريادة are exactly what Saudi families say. Do not replace them with مرشد الصف or a transliteration.
- الفسحة، مشرف الدور، منسوبي القسم، تحويلة، عقد سلوكي، نموذج التعهد، يُعمل بها اعتباراً من، رحلة برية: all native school Arabic.
- `s2.title` "عامان وبرنامج واحد" and `s2.lead` are better than a literal rendering would have been.
- `s5.short` "سنتواصل معكم قبل أن تنخفض الدرجة، لا بعد ذلك" and `s5.partner.p` "حين يلتقي ما نراه في الصف بما ترونه في البيت" are the warmest lines on the page.
- `s3.form.li2` "معروضة مادتين مادتين" and `doc.daily.sb` "حصةً حصة", `s4.progress.li2` "مادةً مادة": idiomatic distributive forms, not "per".
- `s5.mock.p` "حتى لا تكون دورة الصيف أول مرة يعيش فيها الطالب أجواء الاختبار الفعلية" unpacks a compressed English clause properly.
- Imperatives are uniformly second person plural (اطرحوا، ابدأوا، اضغطوا، اطبعوا), and action buttons uniformly use the verbal noun (فتح الملف، عرض الجداول الدراسية). That split is a sound convention; keep it.
- The urgent notice title, "عاجل: الدراسة عن بُعد هذا الأسبوع", is how a Saudi school would actually announce it.
- حجز for confiscation, and "لا إلى السائق ولا إلى أي شخص آخر ينوب عنه" in `s7.phones.li2`.
