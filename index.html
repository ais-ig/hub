03 · Arrival and dismissal

Getting to school and home again

New traffic lanes are in place for 2026/27. Please follow the lane for your campus and stay in your vehicle throughout.

Boys campus gates

MorningGate A, north side. Open 6:45 to 7:00.
DismissalGate B, east side. Lanes 1 to 3 by grade group.
BusesSeparate bay at Gate C. Buses depart 10 minutes after the dismissal bell.

Girls campus gates

MorningGate 1, main entrance. Open 6:50 to 7:05.
DismissalGate 2, west side. Lanes 1 and 2 by grade group.
BusesBus bay at Gate 3. Buses depart 10 minutes after the dismissal bell.

Pick-up, step by step

  1. Arrive no earlier than 10 minutes before the dismissal bell so the lane stays clear.
  2. Display your student pick-up card on the dashboard so staff can read it from the lane.
  3. Stay in the vehicle. Staff walk your child to the car once the card is matched.
  4. If someone else is collecting your child, notify Student Affairs in writing on the same day.

Late pick-up

Students not collected 20 minutes after dismissal wait in the supervised area at reception. Please call the campus office if you are delayed.

05 · Academic follow-up

Following your child's progress

Grades 9 and 10 follow the IGCSE programme. Grades 11 and 12 follow A-Levels, and Grade 12 sits external A2 examinations.

Weekly plans

Published every Thursday for the week ahead, on Schoology under each subject. They list topics, homework, and any assessment falling that week.

Homework

Set on Schoology with a due date. Expect roughly 60 to 90 minutes per evening across subjects in Grades 9 and 10, and more in Grades 11 and 12 as examinations approach.

Assessments

  • Short subject quizzes throughout each term, announced a week ahead.
  • Mid-term examinations in the middle of each term.
  • End-of-term examinations, with a published revision timetable.
  • Grade 12 external A2 examinations follow the awarding body calendar.

Report cards

Three per year, released on MyAIS at the end of each term. A parent meeting follows each release. Exact dates appear under Key dates in section 01 once confirmed.

Attendance

Marked every lesson and visible to you on MyAIS the same day. Report an absence before 7:30 through Student Affairs, and send a medical note on return.

07 · School expectations

What we ask of students and families

Attendance and punctuality

A minimum of 90 percent attendance is expected across the year. Below that, external examination entry can be affected in Grades 11 and 12. Please plan family travel around term dates.

Uniform

Boys: school polo shirt with the emblem, navy trousers, plain black shoes. Sports kit on PE days only.

Girls: school blouse with the emblem, school pinafore or skirt, plain black shoes. Sports kit on PE days only.

Uniform is available from the school shop. A photographic guide is added here once the 2026/27 specification is issued.

Phones and devices

Phones stay switched off and in bags during the school day. To reach your child urgently, call the campus office and we will pass the message on.

Conduct

Students are expected to be respectful, prepared, and honest in their work. Plagiarism and misuse of AI tools in assessed work are treated as academic dishonesty.

Safeguarding

The safety and wellbeing of every student comes before all else. Any concern about a child's safety is acted on the same day. Raise concerns directly with the Head of Section, who is the designated safeguarding contact.

What we ask of parents

  • Check MyAIS and Schoology weekly.
  • Report absence before 7:30 on the day.
  • Keep your phone number and email current with the section office.
  • Attend parent meetings after each report card.
  • Raise concerns early, and start with the subject teacher.
